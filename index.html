Residential

Residential Cleaning

Our standard residential clean is designed to keep your home fresh, tidy and comfortable. It includes:

  • Bathrooms and showers cleaned
  • Toilets sanitised
  • Kitchen benches, sinks and stovetop wiped down
  • Exterior of appliances cleaned
  • General dusting and surface wipe-down
  • Bedrooms and living areas tidied
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Bins emptied
  • Mirrors and accessible glass cleaned
  • Beds made where requested

Additional services such as oven cleaning, refrigerator detailing, internal cupboards, washing, windows and pantry organisation can be added to your booking.

Bond Cleaning

Our detailed bond clean is designed to prepare the property for its final inspection. It includes:

  • Full kitchen clean, including the oven, stovetop and range-hood
  • Bathrooms, showers, toilets and vanities thoroughly cleaned
  • Internal cupboards, drawers and wardrobes cleaned
  • Skirting boards, doors and frames wiped down
  • Ceiling fans, light fittings and air-conditioning vents dusted
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
  • Bedrooms and living areas thoroughly cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Cobwebs removed
  • Laundry area cleaned
  • Property left ready for inspection

Wall cleaning can be added at an additional cost and will be quoted based on the condition and size of the property.
